# 🧴 Skincare Demand Analysis Based on AQI & Air Pollutants

## 📊 Project Overview

This project explores the relationship between **air quality and skincare demand across different regions**.

The dashboard analyzes how variations in **AQI (Air Quality Index)** and specific air pollutants can be associated with different skin-related concerns, and uses those insights to identify the **types of skincare products that may be in higher demand in particular regions**.

The goal is to combine **environmental data, regional analysis, and consumer-oriented skincare insights** into an interactive Power BI dashboard.

> **Note:** The skincare recommendations in this project are analytical/market-oriented insights based on air-quality patterns. They are not medical advice or product prescriptions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze air-quality conditions across different regions.
* Identify pollutants contributing to different AQI conditions.
* Understand potential skin concerns associated with different air-quality environments.
* Map environmental conditions to relevant skincare product categories.
* Identify skincare categories that may have stronger demand in particular regions.
* Present the findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🔎 Key Analysis Areas

### 🌫️ Air Quality

The dashboard focuses on regional AQI conditions and pollutant levels to understand differences in air-quality exposure.

### 🧪 Pollutant-Level Analysis

Rather than looking only at the overall AQI, the project considers specific pollutants and their contribution to regional air-quality conditions.

### 🧴 Potential Skin Concerns

The analysis translates environmental conditions into potential skincare concerns, including:

* Skin dryness
* Irritation and sensitivity
* Dull-looking skin
* Increased cleansing needs
* Environmental-stress-related skincare needs
* Skin-barrier support

### 🛍️ Skincare Demand

Based on the identified environmental conditions, the dashboard maps regions to potentially relevant skincare categories, such as:

* Cleansers
* Moisturizers
* Hydrating products
* Barrier-repair products
* Soothing products
* Antioxidant-focused skincare
* Sunscreen / environmental-protection products

---

## 📈 Dashboard Questions

The dashboard is designed to explore questions such as:

1. Which regions experience higher or lower AQI?
2. Which pollutants are most prominent in different regions?
3. What potential skin concerns may be more relevant in each region?
4. Which skincare categories could see higher demand under those conditions?
5. How can regional air-quality data support skincare-market analysis?

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

This project presents an **analytical hypothesis about potential skincare demand based on air-quality conditions**.

Actual skincare purchasing behavior can be influenced by many other factors, including demographics, climate, income, consumer preferences, brand awareness, seasonality, and existing market demand.

Therefore, this dashboard should be viewed as a **data analytics / business intelligence project**, rather than a medical or dermatological recommendation system.
